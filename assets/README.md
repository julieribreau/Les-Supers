## Prompt flacon

Create a premium photorealistic product photograph for a French natural sports shot brand. Single upright amber brown glass 60 ml shot bottle, black ribbed screw cap, white paper label, on pure white background with soft realistic contact shadow. Exact label typography dark forest green #062B1F: elegant serif brand 'LES SUPERS', smaller 'KICK OFF', large condensed bold 'BOOST & FOCUS', small '60 ML'. Fine flowing dark green contour wave lines across lower label, understated scientific botanical aesthetic. No claims, no ingredient text, no extra objects, no asterisks, no registered trademark. Bottle centered, full bottle visible, fills 80 percent of portrait image. High end editorial studio lighting, authentic glass highlights. Asset only, no webpage or interface.

## Prompt sport et nature

Wide cinematic editorial photograph for a premium natural sports brand website. Adult female trail runner wearing plain dark forest green technical running top, outdoors in the French Alps in soft morning daylight. Waist up profile portrait in left half, looking toward distant mountains on right, composed calm focused expression, realistic skin and tied up brown hair, backlit loose strands. Beautiful lush green mountain valley and lake receding into soft haze, right half offers spacious scenic landscape. Sophisticated muted forest green and cool white palette, natural candid athletic mood, no logos, no text, no UI. Landscape 3:2.
